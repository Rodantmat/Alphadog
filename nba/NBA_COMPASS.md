## 3. Canonical facts (never get these wrong)

**Rules of the project**
1. Additive only — no MLB edits, ever. NBA files live in `nba/`; NBA data lives in `nba_*` schemas only.
2. Tunables live in the database (`nba_config.*`), never hardcoded.
3. Deep research + Gemini consult are mandatory for big decisions; Gemini is a reference, not truth — several of its suggestions were rejected on data (tier globally, drop direction, beta-binomial, close-game star penalty).
4. Operating model: three runs triggered by Claude Coworker scheduled tasks, worker by worker; no orchestrator. Cron for the ladder build is OFF by owner decision until near the season.
5. Standard: "extremely accurate" = calibrated at the leg level (band × direction × rung, and confidence bands that hit their stated rate) on BOTH seasons with the same recipe. A calibrated 75% still loses one time in four.
6. One fixed recipe; every value (tier cutpoints, cells, Platt, HCA, blowout lookup, minutes ratios, dispersion, zero-adjust, factor betas, phase ratios) is computed in-run from history as of the day. Nothing pasted.
7. Goblins/Demons: deferred (board-dependent). Historical prop lines: not free (BigDataBall; owner decision).
7b. BASELINE/ENRICHMENT SPLIT (owner, 2026-09-09): anything derivable from logs/splits/schedule/season tables as of the morning build, or PUBLISHED BEFORE it (official day-before injury report at 5 PM local; referee assignments ~9 AM ET), belongs in the BASELINE; enrichment prices only the same-day residual; board and market are separate stages. Factor lock closed after 5 passes: `NBA_ENRICHMENT_FACTOR_LOCK.md`, config keys `enrichment_factor_lock_status`, `enrichment_subfactor_tree`, `baseline_reassignment_from_enrichment`.

**Data**
8. Sources: `stats.nba.com` via `curl_cffi` on GitHub Actions (Cloudflare egress is blocked by nba.com); committed JSON in `nba/data/`; workers read raw GitHub → Postgres (Hyperdrive). One file per (key, season) — combined files exceeded GitHub's 100 MB limit.
9. `nba/nba_season.py`: `current_season()` (roster/schedule; 2026-27 in Jul–Sep) vs `active_stats_season()` (stats; 2025-26 until Oct 3). Stats scrapers must use the active season or they overwrite real data with zeros.
10. Seasons on disk: 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26 (full-game logs, advanced, team logs, four factors, scoring, quarters Q1–Q4). The 2026-27 schedule (1,266 games) is already in `nba_schedule_current.json`.
11. Daily delta writes `nba_delta_*` files; `nba/sync_season_files_from_delta.py` mirrors them into the season-named files the recipe reads; the delta workflow also refreshes current-season quarters and the schedule.
12. Known source quirks: 3 games of 2025-26 have empty officials at the source (skip list); `boxscoretraditionalv2` unreliable for historical games (v3 used); every quarter file caps at exactly 12.0 minutes (sanity gate).

**Baseline recipe (canonical file `nba/backtest/classification_ladder_v12.py`)**
13. Component model: outcome = projected minutes × per-36 rate / 36; minutes = 3-part mixture (normal / blowout / dud) on a DERIVED static spread (net rating + HCA + rest; r = 0.46 vs final margin, MAE 11.5).
14. Won blowouts are not a penalty (51.9% over-rate vs 49.1%); lost blowouts are (36.9%). B2B is a P(available) effect, not a rate effect.
15. Tiers: rate tier = quantile per-36 rate, max 24 / min 15 per tier, ranked WITHIN role tier; role tiers by competitive-game minutes (IRON_MAN >36, HUS 32–36, STARTER 27–32, ROTATION 21–27, BENCH 15–21, FRINGE <15); variation bands = per-prop line percentiles; direction is a cell dimension.
16. Probabilities: hierarchical empirical cells (tier×role×rung → band×role×rung → band×rung, min 300) over a parametric fallback. Cell mode per prop by evidence: REPLACEMENT for points, rebounds, assists, FGA, 3PA, turnovers, fouls; logit SHIFT with ordering strength λ for 3PM (1.0), blocks/steals/FTM (0.5). Shift applies the finest level only.
17. Prior strength measured, not seeded: points 25, rebounds 40, assists 20, 3PM 25, FGA 15, 3PA 20, blocks 50, steals 125, turnovers 95, fouls 100, FTM 60 (top-decile 20-game regression: STL 17%, PF 14%, TOV 13%, FTA 9%, BLK 6%, REB 4%, FGA 3%).
18. Guards: upper ceiling only (MLB's symmetric floor is a bug on a ladder); Wilson for n<30; Platt per (prop, band, role, rung) with n≥1000, A>0, max shift 0.15, pooled fallback.
19. Band mean-ratio cells only where the sign is consistent across both seasons (rebounds kept; 3PM dropped as regime).
20. Zero-adjusted NegBin for blocks/steals/turnovers/fouls (actual P(0) by mean band from train).
21. Factor layer (owner: in from the start): opponent profile (DEF rating, miss rate, OREB%, TOV%, forced TOV%, FTA rate, paint share, 3PA allowed), pace (geometric mean), home, B2B; betas fit on train in log-rate space. Verdict: real but small (Brier −0.1 to −0.3%); home and B2B ≈ 0.
22. Cross-season carryover (CARRY_N = 8) + season-phase cell (OCT/NOV/MID/APR ratios fit on train seasons with a predecessor). Without them October had zero projections.
23. 3PM is a compound model (tier on 3PA/36; makes | attempts Binomial — var ratio 0.94); FTM likewise on FTA.

Names parse from the Wikipedia season pages; **dates do not** (team infobox formats vary — 1 of 7 parsed) so they are hand-verified. `effective_date` is the first date the NEW coach is in charge — for Brooklyn that is Ollie's first game 2024-02-22, not the 02-19 announcement. Three dates are marked low/medium confidence and should be checked against the game log before use.

Derived tables are built **scoped to need and in monthly blocks**, never one pass over 27M rows. `board_snapshots` uses a compact md5→uuid unique index (1,001 MB) instead of the original 7-column PK (5,577 MB); the successful unique build over 27.06M rows proves zero duplicates. Delete-then-insert must be inside a transaction — an earlier loader emptied `baseline_ladder` for an hour by failing between the two.
66. **DAY-BY-DAY BASELINE HISTORY** (2026-09-12): `nba_score.baseline_history` = 29 stat types × 2 seasons × every game-day × every rung ±10 = **18.78M rows**, keyed (game_date, player_id, game_id, prop, period, line). "Certified" never meant "stored" — the harness discarded per-leg probabilities and kept only calibration summaries; this table is what the engine READS. Builders: `nba/baseline/build_baseline_history.py` / `build_combos_history.py` / `build_periods_history.py` (patchers over the three certified recipes; anchors asserted — the recipes differ in shape), loader `nba/load_baseline_history.py`, workflows `nba-baseline-history.yml` / `nba-combos-history.yml` / `nba-periods-history.yml`. Periods cover 152 of 163 game-days (quarter files). Coverage of the real PrizePicks board season-wide: **89.6%** (`nba/check_season_coverage.py`).

Added fgm/fta/dreb to the singles recipe — **certified both seasons** (all three per-prop tables must be extended: PROPS config, VBANDS_ALL, and the factor-feature map). **oreb FAILED** (−21 pp on LOW/more near a 0.5–1.5 anchor; zero-inflated) → excluded until retuned. Dunks need play-by-play. The historical BOARD only has 13 of those types (Odds API archive) — training/evaluation against a real board is possible for 13, live scoring for all. Fantasy score = 1/1.2/1.5/3/3/−1 for BOTH Underdog (official) and PrizePicks; DraftKings' table (1.25 reb, 2 stl/blk, −0.5 tov, +0.5 3PM, DD/TD bonuses) is the trap.
68. STAGE ASSIGNMENT (owner 2026-09-11/12): anything knowable before 2:30 PM PT is computed in PHASE 1 (baseline/delta, may take hours, can start ~3 AM PT after prior-night box scores); PHASE 2 (2:30 → first tip) handles ONLY the day-of report, projected-lineup delta, the 2:45 board and market, and rescores only the legs those touch. Latest pre-window input is the **1 PM ET game-day injury report (~10 AM PT)** → scenario precompute starts ~10:30 AM PT; referee assignments post ~6–7 AM PT → **baseline stage**. Registry column `compute_stage` (15 phase-1 / 17 phase-2 / 2 live-only / 2 not-mined). Scenario design: per GAME, the joint availability set of both teams (uncertain players resolve IN/OUT; ≤64 scenarios, usually 8–16); precompute the full matrix per scenario (~0.5–1M rows/day, ~50–100 MB, delete all but the selected one at 2:30). PrizePicks averages 4,503 board legs/day (max 8,700); plan for 10–20k/day live across five apps; the phase-1 matrix is ~72k leg-probabilities/day regardless.
70. **REDISTRIBUTION IS AN ALLOCATION, NEVER AN OBSERVATIONAL DELTA** (2026-09-12). A team plays 240 minutes (+25/OT) regardless, so vacated minutes are redistributed BY DEFINITION and the model form must encode it. Five panels failed the conservation gate (0.08 / −0.05 / −0.37 / 0.25 / 0.49 instead of ~1.0), each for a different surface reason — minutes floor, `pair_games >= 5`, the `leaguedashlineups` 2,000-row cap, `single_absence` (218 of ~1,150 team-games), deltas vs a personal rolling mean — and EVERY one removed the players who absorb the minutes. A rolling mean already contains the season's earlier absences, so a player averaging 20 min BECAUSE the star is out shows delta ~0. **Conservation (do the shares sum to 1) is the gate; nothing is fitted until it passes.**
71. FACTOR A2 SHIPPED: allocator share = `exp(0.2214 + 0.3953·log(as-of min) + 0.5160·log(recent-5) + 0.0043·log1p(games))` normalised × team minutes (`nba/fit_minutes_allocator.py`); the factor is the DIFFERENCE OF TWO ALLOCATIONS — over {played + ruled out} vs {played} (`nba/build_redistribution_factors.py` → `nba_score.redistribution_factors`, 51,806 rows, conservation 1.0015). Cold start = bench prior 8 min, NEVER dropped. Held out on absence games: minutes MAE **4.641 with outs vs 6.186 IGNORING outs vs 5.029 as-of mean** — allocating while ignoring absences is WORSE than doing nothing. Per-prop gains pra +0.347 / pts_reb +0.281 / pts_ast +0.253 / points +0.097 → **15 of 19 props**; skip stocks/blocks/steals/fouls. Two-season min_mult 1.3228 / 1.3147.
72. REJECTED WITH TESTS: **rate response** (held-out points MAE 4.753 vs 4.733 minutes-only — worse; usage is already carried by minutes + baseline rate, a separate multiplier double-counts) and **B4 opponent availability in both formulations** (v1 opponent minutes/count 0 of 19 props; v2 expected-defender-quality 0 of 11, betas −0.056…+0.029 after fixing a scoping bug). Opponent defence is ALREADY in the baseline; B4 was only the same-day residual and there is none. The retracted +6.5% observational figure was blowout/garbage-time contamination. Open sub-case: `blocks_against_vulnerability`.
73. FACTOR N1 MEASURED at the 2:30 PM PT cutoff — and it corrects the league's own table: Out 0.001; **Doubtful 0.005 (league says 0.25 — doubtful means OUT)**; Questionable **0.552 rotation / 0.312 fringe**; Available 0.956 and Probable 0.961 for rotation players (the 0.828 headline is deep-bench DNP-CD, not scratches). By reason: two-way 0.271, back 0.488, soft tissue 0.539. By team: ATL 0.326 … GSW 0.676. NBA Dec-2025 overhaul fixed nominal probabilities and added AVAILABLE mid-season.
74. A2 UNDER WINDOW INFORMATION: rebuilding the absence input from the 2:30 PM report with N1 fractional weights gives minutes MAE **6.210 none / 4.580 window / 4.368 perfect** → **window knowledge captures 88% of perfect**; uncertainty costs 0.21 minutes. Any factor fitted on post-game truth must be re-tested this way before its value is believed.
75. LIVE BOARD ARCHIVER (`nba/archive_live_boards.py` + `nba-board-archive.yml`): boards land in `nba_market.board_snapshots` in the same shape as the two-season backfill — without it the scrapers just overwrite `boards/<app>_<sport>_current.json` and the live season produces no queryable history. Synthesises a stable `event_id` (live boards have no Odds API id, and the column is NOT NULL); **isolates parser failures** so one app cannot cost the others their archive. Verified on live MLB: Underdog 5,281 legs, Sleeper 1,276. **Fliff is blocked UPSTREAM** — the scraper emits empty `player` and `line` on all 4,592 legs; fix belongs in `scrape_fliff_board.py`.
76. CALIBRATION IS RE-CHECKABLE FROM STORED DATA (`nba/check_prop_calibration.py`, maintenance task `calibration`): any prop in `baseline_history` is graded against the box scores by confidence band. Previously a prop's verdict existed only inside an ephemeral harness log. Validated against points (worst band 0.8 pp). **oreb**: `SHIFT_LAMBDA = 0.5` (matching blocks/steals/ftm) took its worst band from **−21.2 pp to −2.5 pp** — borderline, needs the second-season confirmation before being called certified.
78. **PROP CONFIDENCE TIERS** (config `prop_confidence_policy`, 2026-09-13): not every prop earns the same trust. **certified** = passes leg-level calibration on BOTH seasons (worst band ≤ 2.5 pp) → full confidence. **penalized** = the mean is sound but band calibration fails → confidence downgraded one tier, BARRED from the top-confidence slip tier, at most one such leg per slip, skipped entirely if the edge is below the standard threshold. **excluded** = the mean is wrong or unmodelled → no legs. Dropping a prop whose mean is right over-corrects; treating it as certified under-corrects.
80. **FULL PROP RELIABILITY AUDIT** (2026-09-13, config `prop_reliability_audit_2026_09_13`, tool `nba/score_prop_reliability.py` via maintenance task `reliability`): all **30 props scored on one scale** from stored data — n-weighted ECE, worst band, Brier, and lift over a base-rate model, both seasons. Result: **27 certified, 3 penalized, 0 unverified**. Penalties are DERIVED (`ECE − certified median 0.20 pp`), never declared: **fantasy_score −0.3**, **double_double −0.4**, **oreb −0.1**. Before this audit TEN props (5.4M rows, a third of the table) had NO verdict because the scorer silently skipped anything without a matching box-score column — the 7 period props, fantasy_score, double_double and stocks. Unverified is not the same as fine; extending it caught two props that would have shipped as certified. Period props are graded by wiring in the quarter box scores (`nba_player_game_log_q{1..4}_{season}.json`).
81. **STRATEGIC FINDING — the board's deepest markets are its least exploitable.** Lift over a base-rate model: threes_made_q1 **27.3%** (best Brier in the system, 0.0232), steals 26.9%, assists_q1 26.1%, oreb 25.8%, rebounds_q1 25.1%, stocks 25.0% — against **pra 7.5%**, pts_reb 8.3%, pts_ast 9.2% and **fantasy_score 5.2%** (our highest-volume prop, 994,879 rows, and the lowest lift we have). A combo averages three noisy counts together, diluting the role signal that makes a single short-period stat forecastable. **The slip engine should weight toward high-LIFT props, not high-volume ones.**
 The mean-level defect is fixed and measured: `k_stab` 60 → **4** and `alpha` 0.08 → **0.15** cut the worst anchor-band bias **0.241 → 0.045** (81%), validated by a monotone k sweep (k=3 and k=5 both 0.045; k=20 gives 0.379) with the best MAE at low k. What remains is DISPERSION, not the mean: the residual calibration misses ALTERNATE SIGN and cluster in the thinnest bands, which is tail noise from fitting ONE global negative-binomial shape to a bursty zero-inflated stat (0,0,0,4) across both bench players and elite crashers. Rejected on evidence first: shift-lambda 0.5 (passed one season, failed the other), the textbook opportunity basis ORB/(FGA−FGM) × expected team missed shots (moved bias ~0.02), and minutes-tier shrinkage (FLIPPED the sign, +0.192/−0.658 — a minutes tier mixes crash-first centers with wings who never crash). **What worked: shrink toward an ARCHETYPE prior defined by DEFENSIVE rebound rate** — correlated with size and role but NOT the target, so the grouping does not leak — with LIGHT shrinkage. The method should transfer to other bursty low-count stats.

## 5. Procedures (exact)

- **Daily delta**: bump `nba/TRIGGER_NBA_DAILY_DELTA.txt` → `run_job(NBA_DAILY_DELTA_WORKER, "run")` → `run_job(NBA_STATIC_MEASURE_TYPES_WORKER, "run", extra={"file_prefix":"nba/data/nba_delta_"})` → `NBA_STATIC_STARTER_STATUS_WORKER` → `NBA_STATIC_GAME_OFFICIALS_WORKER`. The delta workflow itself already mirrors season files, refreshes quarters and the schedule.
- **Baseline ladder for a day**: set `asof: YYYY-MM-DD` (and `replay: 1` for a past day) in `nba/TRIGGER_NBA_BASELINE.txt`, push; wait ~2 h; then `run_job(NBA_BASELINE_LADDER_WORKER, "run", extra={"asof":"YYYY-MM-DD"})`; verify with `SELECT prop, COUNT(*) FROM nba_score.baseline_ladder WHERE asof='...' GROUP BY prop`.
- **Backtest**: bump `nba/TRIGGER_NBA_BACKTEST.txt`; reports commit to `nba/backtest/reports/`. Locally: `BT_TRAIN=2023-24,2024-25 BT_TEST=2025-26 BT_PROPS=points,rebounds python3 nba/backtest/classification_ladder_v12.py` (≤540 s per pair); holdout = `BT_TRAIN=2023-24 BT_TEST=2024-25`.
- **Quarter data refresh**: bump `nba/TRIGGER_NBA_PERIODS.txt` (3 seasons) or rely on the daily delta (`PERIODS_SEASONS=1`).
- **Weekly static**: bump `nba/TRIGGER_NBA_SCRAPE.txt` then the static workers; backfill worker `{"mode":"weekly"}` loads splits + career.
- **Reading results**: `summarize.py` pattern — ladder max |gap| over 13 rungs, confidence bands with n≥1000 over 2.5pp, worst band×direction×rung cells (n≥500, >2.5pp).
